01

DESIGN &
ENGINEERING

MPS engineers each project with precision, from the first layout sketch to the final breaker. The team covers multi-disciplinary solar plant engineering so execution is smooth and long-term performance is protected.

The process starts with optimized plant layouts that consider land contours, shadow analysis, and yield. It then moves into electrical and structural design, balancing performance, safety, and cost.

  • Voltage drop analysis and optimized AC/DC cable sizing.
  • Protection system design, breaker sizing, and fault current calculations.
  • Structure validation for wind loads, soil conditions, and stability checks.
  • GA drawings, SLDs, and construction-ready documentation.
02

BATTERY ENERGY
STORAGE SYSTEMS

MPS provides complete BESS design and engineering for grid stability, load shifting, backup, frequency regulation, solar smoothing, and renewable integration.

The team maps the application, sizes energy requirements, designs the architecture, and builds the control strategy across AC-coupled and DC-coupled systems.

  • Load and autonomy calculations.
  • Battery sizing using C-rating and daily cycle expectations.
  • System voltage and inverter matching.
  • Fuse and breaker coordination for protection design.
  • Integration with solar PV and diesel gensets in hybrid setups.
03

TECHNICAL DOCS
& TENDER SUPPORT

MPS prepares the documentation that helps asset owners, consultants, government bodies, and procurement teams define scope clearly and select EPC partners with confidence.

The work covers feasibility through final EPC selection, including technical packages, bid comparisons, clarifications, and evaluation support.

  • Detailed BOMs and equipment schedules.
  • SLDs, GA drawings, and layout drawings.
  • Design basis reports, performance simulations, and yield forecasts.
  • Feasibility reports and grid compliance studies.
  • Tender documents with scope, guarantees, and evaluation criteria.
  • EPC filtering, scoring matrix support, and bid comparisons.
04

INSTALLATION &
COMMISSIONING

MPS provides on-ground installation and commissioning support shaped around the project model, whether that means turnkey execution or targeted last-mile commissioning assistance.

Engineers, supervisors, and technical specialists help ensure the plant is built correctly, tested properly, and handed over with the right approvals and documentation.

  • Site engineers and supervisors for installation oversight.
  • Turnkey execution of small to mid-scale projects.
  • Pre-commissioning checks and inverter or grid compliance validation.
  • SCADA, protection system, and communication setup.
  • DISCOM, CEIG, and grid authority coordination.
  • Performance testing, quality audits, and handover documentation.
05

O&M, REPORTING
& PREDICTIVE CARE

MPS keeps solar assets performing with O&M programs that combine routine care, remote monitoring, data-led performance analysis, and predictive maintenance.

The goal is simple: catch issues early, protect uptime, and keep yield optimized across utility, commercial, and industrial solar plants.

  • Preventive maintenance plans based on OEM standards and site conditions.
  • Daily, monthly, and annual KPI reporting.
  • Predictive diagnostics with inverter data, IV curves, and weather correlation.
  • Root cause analysis for technical losses and underperformance.
  • Cleaning, vegetation, physical inspections, and event response.
  • Warranty coordination, spares inventory, and ticketing systems.
06

BALANCE OF SYSTEM
SUPPLY

Beyond design and execution, MPS supplies high-quality BOS components through certified manufacturers and global suppliers, with engineering compatibility and delivery timing kept in view.

The supply model supports spec-to-spec matching, logistics planning, and site coordination so construction can move cleanly without avoidable delays.

  • DC, AC, earthing, and communication cables.
  • DCDBs, ACDBs, AJBs, and custom LT panels.
  • MC4 connectors, cable trays, and junction boxes.
  • HDG, pre-galvanized, aluminum, and module mounting structures.
  • Inverter stations, combiner boxes, and surge protection devices.
  • Transformers, isolators, RMUs, SCADA enclosures, weather stations, and instruments.
